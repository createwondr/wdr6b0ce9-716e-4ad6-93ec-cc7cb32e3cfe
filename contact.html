Contact

Have a question, found a bug, or want to share an idea? We'd love to hear from you.

1. Email Us

Reach us at support@createwondr.io and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

2. What We Can Help With

  • Questions about using Wondr
  • Bug reports and feedback
  • Plans, payments and AI credits
  • Account requests, such as deleting your account and cloud save
  • Reports of prohibited content
  • Missing or outdated credits on the Attribution page

3. Reporting a Bug

To help us fix it faster, please include:

  • what you were doing when it happened
  • your browser and device
  • a screenshot or a short screen recording, if you can

4. Billing

Pro members can update their card, see their orders or cancel their subscription in Settings, under Manage Billing. For anything else about payments, email us.

5. Find Answers Faster

Many answers are already in the Tutorials, including Troubleshooting and Keyboard shortcuts, and in the FAQs on our homepage.

6. Follow Wondr

See what's new and what others create with Wondr on Instagram, TikTok, Threads, YouTube and Reddit.
