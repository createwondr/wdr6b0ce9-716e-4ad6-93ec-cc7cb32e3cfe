About Wondr

Wondr is an easy-to-use, powerful 2D and 3D graphic design web app built for endless creative possibilities. It runs right in your web browser, so there's nothing to install. Log in and the workspace becomes your creative world.

1. What You Can Do

Wondr brings the tools of a full design app into one simple workspace:

  • Design in 2D, then switch to 3D view in one click.
  • Preview your designs on 3D models, such as a t-shirt, a mug, a laptop or a phone.
  • Cut out any subject from your photos with AI background removal.
  • Style text, images and shapes with strokes, gradients, glows and shadows.
  • Recolor each part of your SVG files and icons.
  • Fill your designs with patterns, elements, graphics and stock photos.
  • Start fast from templates, and revert your actions whenever you want.
  • Export your work, or save the full project as a Wondr file (.wdr) to open again anytime.

2. Who's Behind Wondr

Wondr is a creative work of Ozydeus, a solo designer and developer based in the Philippines. Every part of Wondr, from the interface to the code behind it, is designed and built by one person.

3. Why Wondr

Wondr is an independent project for creators. It is made to be simple enough for your first design and powerful enough for your best one. By supporting it, you're helping shape its future and build a better creative experience.

4. Your Work Stays Yours

Everything you export is watermark-free and yours to use for personal or commercial work, including designs made from templates. You own the copyright to your original work, and your designs are never shared with advertisers. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for the details.

5. Plans

Wondr has two plans. Pro is a monthly subscription with full access and 50 AI credits daily. Founding Member is one payment for lifetime access, with limited seats only. See Pricing for everything included.

6. Learn and Get in Touch

New to Wondr? The Tutorials walk you through every tool, from your first design to your first export. For questions, help or feedback, visit the Contact page or reach us at support@createwondr.io.
