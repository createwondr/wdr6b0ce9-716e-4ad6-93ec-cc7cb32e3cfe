Thank you for using Wondr

 Wondr was founded by Ozydeus, a solo indie designer and developer. It started as a small project, built from a simple idea: to create something useful, meaningful, and to extend human creativity. It was personally designed, developed, and funded from the very beginning, with no investors, no large team, and no millions behind it. Just one person, a computer, countless hours, and the belief that even a humble idea can grow into something worth sharing.

 The app is built with HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript, the fundamental building blocks of the web. Its philosophy is inspired by the Wonders of the World, remarkable creations imagined and built from the ground up, piece by piece, long before modern frameworks and standards existed. There is something inspiring about returning to the fundamentals and discovering how much can be achieved with them.

 Wondr is dedicated to Wanda Teresa Dela Cruz, a mother who shared her love with her children unconditionally and provided for the people she cared about. This work carries a part of that legacy forward. A product of love, persistence, passion and the joy of building something simply because you believe it can spread wonder.

 We are excited to see what you can be and what you can create.

 Welcome to Wondr!
