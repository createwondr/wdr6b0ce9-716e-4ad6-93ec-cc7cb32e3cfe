4. Founding Member Plan

Founding Members receive a smaller daily credit allowance than Monthly Subscribers. Founding Members pay once for permanent access, while Monthly Subscribers pay for each month of access, and the higher daily allowance reflects that ongoing payment.

5. Monthly Subscriber Plan

Background-Removal Credits

  • 50 credits per day, refreshed daily (see Section 6)

6. Background-Removal Credits

Background removal is the one feature that uses credits; everything else in the studio is included with your plan. Both background-removal tools, “Soft edge” and “Hard edge”, share the same daily allowance.

Daily Allowance

  • Founding Member: 20 credits per day
  • Monthly Subscriber: 50 credits per day

Cost per Removal

Each removal uses credits based on the longest side of the image:

  • up to 1024 px: 1 credit
  • up to 2048 px: 2 credits
  • up to 4096 px: 4 credits
  • images larger than 4096 px need to be resized before removal

How Credits Work

  • credits are used only when a removal is completed successfully
  • your allowance refreshes every day at 00:00 UTC
  • unused credits do not carry over to the next day
  • if you use all of your credits, background removal becomes available again at the next refresh, and the rest of the studio stays fully available
  • credits have no cash value and cannot be transferred, sold, or exchanged

We recommend exporting your important work regularly so you always have your own copy.
