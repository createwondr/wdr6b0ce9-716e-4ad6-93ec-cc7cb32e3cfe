Privacy Policy

Updated: November 21, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Wondr (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our website and design tools (“Service”). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.


We operate from the Republic of the Philippines, and this Policy follows the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Google Login Information

When you sign in using Google Login, we receive the following information from your Google account:

  • your email address

  • your display name

  • your Google profile ID


We do not receive your Google password at any time.


1.2 Device Information (Fingerprinting)

To enforce our one-device-at-a-time security rule, we collect device identification details generated by Thumbmark.js.

  • device fingerprint

  • browser settings

  • operating system

  • session identifiers


We do not collect or track precise location information.


1.3 Technical Logs

The Service automatically logs authentication and security-related events in the cloud, which may include:

  • account status

  • device fingerprint

  • date and time logs

  • session creation and expiration events


1.4 Payment Information

Payments are processed by independent third-party providers. We do not store or handle any sensitive payment details.

  • We do not store credit card numbers

  • We do not store billing addresses

  • We only receive confirmation of payment status (successful or failed)


1.5 Background Removal Usage

To maintain proper usage and system performance, we record your background-removal credit activity.

  • credit reset timing

  • number of used and remaining credits

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or access your creative content or design files in any form. This includes:

  • your design files

  • drafts and works-in-progress

  • exported outputs such as images or PDFs

  • canvas or editor data

  • uploaded assets or templates

  • content generated inside the editor

3. Storage of User Content

Your design data and drafts are stored only on your device, using browser storage mechanisms such as Local Storage, IndexedDB, or Cache.

  • drafts never leave your device

  • we cannot access or restore deleted local data

  • clearing browser data permanently deletes autosave project

4. No AI Training or Dataset Use

We do not use any user content for AI training, dataset building, or machine learning purposes.

  • we do not scan your designs

  • we do not analyze your artwork

  • we do not use your images for algorithm development

5. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to operate, secure, and maintain the Service.

  • account authentication

  • enforcing one-session-at-a-time login rules

  • preventing account sharing and abuse

  • managing background-removal credits

  • ensuring service performance and security

  • communicating important service updates

  • compliance with legal requirements

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We only share information with essential service providers:

  • Google Login for authentication

  • Supabase for authorization and security logs

  • Payment processors for completing transactions

  • Legal authorities when required by law

7. Cookies and Local Storage

The Service uses essential cookies and browser storage solely for functionality and security.

  • authentication cookies via Google Login

  • session cookies via Supabase

  • cache storage for autosaving feature


We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or behavioral analytics cookies.

8. Data Retention

We retain only the information necessary to operate the Service effectively and securely.

  • device fingerprints

  • login and authentication logs

  • account information

  • blacklist records (if applicable)


Payment providers may retain records as required by financial regulations.

9. Blacklisting and Fraud Prevention

To prevent abuse, we may temporarily or permanently blacklist emails, devices, or accounts involved in suspicious or prohibited activity.

  • account sharing

  • device spoofing or tampering

  • repeated abuse or fraud attempts

10. Children’s Privacy

The Service is intended for users aged 13 and above.

  • we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13

  • accounts identified as belonging to users under 13 will be deleted

11. Security Measures

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your data.

  • Google Login authentication

  • Supabase authorization

  • HTTPS encryption

  • device fingerprint security

  • access control

  • IP-based abuse monitoring

  • rate limiting

12. International Data Processing

Because our authentication and hosting providers may operate globally, your information may be stored or processed outside the Philippines.

13. Your Rights Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • right to access

  • right to correct or update

  • right to delete

  • right to object to processing

  • right to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the Service means you accept the updated terms.
