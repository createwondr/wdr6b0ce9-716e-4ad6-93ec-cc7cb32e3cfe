FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is Wondr?

Wondr is an easy-to-use, powerful 2D and 3D graphic design web app. Make social posts, posters, banners, digital designs and product visuals with layers and elements. Use our AI background removal feature, explore 3D mockups, and more.

Who's behind Wondr?

Wondr is a creative work of Ozydeus, a solo designer and developer based in the Philippines.

Can I use the app directly?

Yes, indeed. Nothing to install. Log in with your account and the workspace becomes your creative world. It works best on a web browser.

Can I use my designs commercially?

Yes, definitely. Everything you export is watermark-free and yours to use for personal or commercial work, including designs made from templates. For stock photos, see the Pexels license.

What about my work ownership?

By default, you own the copyright to your original work.

How do AI credits work?

AI credits are used by Wondr's AI features. They refresh daily, and unused credits don't carry over.

Can I keep a copy of my project?

Yes, absolutely. Besides cloud autosave, you can save the full project to your computer as a Wondr .wdr file and share or open it again anytime.

How do I reach Wondr?

For further questions, assistance, or bug reports, reach us at support@createwondr.io and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.
