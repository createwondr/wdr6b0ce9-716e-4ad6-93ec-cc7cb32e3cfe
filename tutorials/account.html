Account and sign-in

Sign in

Wondr uses your Google account. On the sign-in page, click Continue with Google and choose your account in the window that opens. Wondr only receives your email address and Google account ID, and never sees your Google password. After you sign in:

  • With an active plan, you go straight to the app.
  • Without a plan, you go to the plans page to choose one.

Note: Sign-in opens in a small pop-up window. If nothing happens, allow pop-ups for createwondr.io in your browser.

Your account details

Open Settings to see Account & Billing:

  • AI Credits left today.
  • Email, partly hidden, for example j••••n@gmail.com.
  • Member since, the date your access started.
  • Access, and Renews on or Ends on, the date your plan renews or ends. Founding Members see a Founding Member badge instead.
  • Auto-renew, to turn your Pro plan's monthly renewal on or off. See Settings.

One device at a time

You can be signed in on one device at a time. When you sign in on a new device, your other device is signed out. Your autosave is always the work of your latest session.

Sign out

In the app, open Settings and click Log Out. Clear Cache also signs you out after clearing Wondr's stored files.

Your designs and your privacy

  • Your autosave is private to your account.
  • Wondr files are encrypted. Wondr keeps no copy of the files you save to your computer.
  • Wondr uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

See the Privacy Policy for the full details.

Delete your account

Reach us at support@createwondr.io. Deleting an account is permanent: your account, your autosave and your access are removed, and payments already made are not refunded.

Reach us

For any concerns, you may reach us at support@createwondr.io.
