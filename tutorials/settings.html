Settings

The Settings menu holds your app preferences and your account details.

UI Settings

  • Snapping: when on, a layer you move or resize snaps to the edges, centers and gaps of other layers. On by default.
  • Snap Sensitivity: from 1 to 20. Higher values snap from further away. The default is 5.
  • Snap Line Color: the color of the snap guides and of the drop line when you reorder layers. Aquamarine by default.
  • Selection Color: the color of the box you drag to select layers.
  • Handler Color: the color of the handles on a selected layer.

Click a color swatch to pick a color, or type a hex value. Click the reset icon next to UI Settings to put everything back to its default.

Note: Wondr remembers these settings in this browser, so they are still set the next time you open the app, even after you close it. Another browser or device starts with the defaults.

Account & Billing

  • AI Credits: credits left today for AI background removal. They refresh every day. See Plans and AI credits.
  • Email: your sign-in email, partly hidden.
  • Member since: the date your plan started.
  • Access: your plan. Founding Members see a Founding Member badge instead, because their access does not expire.
  • Renews on or Ends on: the date your Pro plan renews, or the date it ends when auto-renew is off.
  • Auto-renew: on by default for the Pro plan. See Auto-renew below.
  • Manage Billing: opens your billing page in a new tab. See Manage Billing below.

Founding Members see their badge in place of Access, Renews on, Auto-renew and Manage Billing:

Auto-renew

Your Pro plan renews every month on its own, so your access never stops by accident. The Auto-renew switch is in Account & Billing, just above Manage Billing.

  • Turn Auto-renew off to stop future payments. Wondr asks you to confirm first. You keep full access until the date under Ends on, and payments already made are not refunded.
  • Turn it back on anytime before that date. No confirmation needed.

Tip: When auto-renew is off and your subscription ends soon, you may see a daily reminder at the top of the app.

Manage Billing

Manage Billing opens your billing page from our payment provider in a new tab. It shows for Pro members who paid through Wondr's checkout. There you can update your card, see your orders or cancel your subscription.

Cancelling there stops your subscription at once on the billing page, but you keep full access to Wondr until the date under Ends on, and Wondr hides the Auto-renew switch when you come back to the app. To subscribe again later, choose a plan on the Pricing page.

Clear Cache and Log Out

  • Clear Cache deletes the resources Wondr has stored in your browser, such as templates, fonts and 3D environments, so fresh copies download. It also logs you out.
  • Log Out signs you out of Wondr.

Tip: Try Clear Cache if templates or fonts look out of date. Your autosaved design stays safe in the cloud. See Troubleshooting.
