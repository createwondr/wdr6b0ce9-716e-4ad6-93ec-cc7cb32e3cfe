Plans and AI credits

Every Wondr plan unlocks the full studio: every feature, template and asset, with exports that have no watermark. There are two ways to get access.

Monthly

  • Full access for one month at a time.
  • Pay per month. Your plan does not renew automatically, so you are never charged without choosing to.
  • 50 AI credits every day for background removal.
  • Future updates and features included.

Founding Member

  • Lifetime, full access to every feature, template and asset, for as long as Wondr runs.
  • One payment. No renewal and no recurring charges.
  • 50 AI credits every day for background removal.
  • Future updates and features included.
  • A Founding Member badge on your account.

Founding Member seats are limited.

AI credits

Background removal feature uses credits. Everything else in the studio is included with your plan.

  • Your credits refresh every day at 00:00 UTC. Unused credits do not carry over.
  • Soft edge and hard edge removal share the same daily credits.
  • You are only charged when a removal finishes successfully.

What a removal costs depends on the longest side of the image:

  • Up to 1024 pixels: 1 credit.
  • Up to 2048 pixels: 2 credits.
  • Up to 4096 pixels: 4 credits.
  • Larger images cannot be processed. Resize them first.

See your balance in Settings under AI Credits. It updates after each removal.

Tip: If you see Daily credits have maxed out but still have a few credits left, the image costs more than you have. Try a smaller version of it, or wait for the daily refresh.

When a Monthly plan ends

  • You may access the premium features once you renew your subscription.
  • Your last saved design is kept safely in your account.

Wondr files open in any Wondr account with an active plan.
