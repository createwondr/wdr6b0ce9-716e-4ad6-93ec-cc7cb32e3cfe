Learn / Saving and exporting

Autosave

Wondr saves your design to your private cloud space while you work. There is no button to press.

How it works

  • The autosave keeps your full undo history, your uploads and your project title.
  • Your account has one autosave: your latest work. Whatever you change on the canvas is what gets saved, so working on a template or another file replaces it.

Note: Autosave needs an internet connection.

Resume your work

When you sign in and an autosave is waiting, the bottom of the Designs menu asks Resume your work?

  • Okay loads your last autosave onto the canvas, with its title and undo history.
  • Close starts fresh. Your next change is autosaved in place of the older work.

While the prompt is unanswered, autosave waits so it cannot overwrite the work it is offering.

Warning: Making any change, opening a template or opening a Wondr file before you click Okay counts as starting fresh, and the older autosave is replaced. If you want your last work back, click Okay first.

When a save fails

If a save cannot reach the server, you see an error message. Check your online connection and try again.

When your plan ends

You can still download your autosave or email us at support@createwondr.io to keep working. See Plans and AI credits.

Working in two places

You can be signed in on one device at a time. If you sign in somewhere else, the previous session ends and will automatically log out. See Account and sign-in.

Tip: Autosave is your safety net, not an archive. To keep versions of a project, save Wondr files locally from the Save menu.
