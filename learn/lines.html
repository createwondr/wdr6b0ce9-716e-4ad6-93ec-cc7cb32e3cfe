Lines

Lines come from the Shapes category of the Elements menu. A selected line has three tabs: Basic Properties, Line Properties and Line Style.

Line Properties

  • Heads: the two menus at the top set the start and the end of the line. Choose a flat or round end, or an arrow, circle, diamond, square or triangle, each as an outline or filled.
  • Color: a solid color for the line and its heads.
  • Width: the line's thickness. The heads grow with it.
  • Dash and Gap: make a dashed line. A Dash of 0 gives a solid line.
  • Cap: rounds the ends of the line and of each dash.

Corner shows a dash because lines have no corners.

Line Style

Pick Glow or Shadow in Choose a Style, or Remove to take it off. They work like the image styles. See Image properties.

Move, resize and rotate a line

  • A selected line has a handle at each end. Drag one to make the line longer or shorter.
  • To angle the line, use Rotation in Basic properties, or hover just past one of its ends until the cursor curves, then drag.

Note: Lines are straight. There are no curved or multi-point lines.
