Everything you need to go from first idea to finished piece lives on one canvas. No plugins to hunt for, no extra apps to switch between.

3D mockups

See your design on the real thing.

Wrap your artwork onto a 3D product, turn it to the best angle and light it with studio, outdoor or filmic environments. Show clients the finished product before anything is printed.

  • Rotate, offset and scale the print area
  • Indoor, outdoor, warm, cool and filmic lighting
  • Background blur and exposure controls

AI background removal

Clean cutouts in one click.

Lift a product or a person out of any photo and keep working with it as a layer. Choose a soft edge for hair and fur, or a hard edge for crisp objects.

  • Soft edge and hard edge modes
  • Undo and redo like any other edit
  • Daily AI credits included with every plan

Layers and vectors

Every detail, under your control.

Stack layers, mask images into any shape, blend them together and edit vector paths point by point. Precise numbers when you need them, free dragging when you don't.

  • Masks, blend modes and filters
  • Vector path editing with fill, stroke and dash
  • Full undo history, saved with your project

Typography

Type with presence.

Pick from a large library of Google Fonts and give your words depth with outline, glow and shadow styles you can tune to the pixel. Your fonts travel with the project.

  • Searchable font library
  • Outline, glow and shadow styles
  • Emojis and symbols built in

Patterns

Patterns in seconds.

Turn any shape into a seamless pattern. Adjust spacing and scale, add a random spin, cascade it in any direction and finish it with a vignette or bloom.

  • Spacing, scale and random spin
  • Cascade, frame, vignette and bloom styles
  • Blend patterns with the rest of your design

FAQs

Questions,
answered.

Can't find what you need? Write to support@createwondr.io and we'll help.

What is Wondr?

Wondr is a design studio that runs in your browser. You can create social posts, posters, banners and product visuals with layers, vectors, type, patterns, AI background removal and 3D mockups, then export them ready to use.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Sign in with your Google account and the studio opens in your browser. It works best on a desktop or laptop computer; on phones some features may be limited.

Can I use my designs commercially?

Yes. Everything you export is watermark-free and yours to use for personal or commercial work, including designs made from templates. Stock photos follow the Pexels license.

Who owns what I make?

You do. We claim no rights over your designs, and we never use them to train AI models or to build datasets.

How do AI credits work?

Background removal is the only feature that uses credits; everything else is included with your plan. Credits refresh every day at 00:00 UTC, and unused credits don't carry over.

Why is the price different in my country?

Prices are set per country so Wondr stays fair wherever you are. They are shown in US dollars, and your country is estimated from your connection.

Can I keep a copy of my project?

Yes. Besides cloud autosave, you can save the full project to your computer as a Wondr file and open it again in any account with an active plan.
